# Code of conduct

MoobFS welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Be respectful, discuss ideas with evidence, give constructive feedback and make
space for questions and learning. English and Portuguese participation are welcome.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption,
and sharing someone else's private information without consent are unacceptable.
This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces
maintained by the project, and to conduct when representing the project publicly.

Report concerns privately to **claudio@studiomoob.com**. Include relevant links and
only the personal information needed to understand the report. Reports are handled
with discretion; information may be shared as needed to investigate and respond.
Do not publish reports in an issue. If the report concerns the current maintainer,
use GitHub's platform reporting tools; this small project has no independent panel.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict access
or ban participants, based on severity and context. They should explain moderation
decisions to affected participants where appropriate. Appeals may be sent to the
same address. No guaranteed response time is promised.

This is the project's own conduct policy, not an unmodified third-party covenant.
